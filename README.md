## About

An entry for the make.fun competition
[Sum up 20 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261120).
The program is a one-step copy: `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only)
copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `/output`) as `entry.png`.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

## The image

A newspaper front page headed **"Friday 20 November 2026 — The World
in One Day"**, with five stories from the day, each told by a picture and a
headline:

1. **Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen** — the first-ever three-way
   summit of the US, Chinese and Russian leaders, held on the sidelines
   of the APEC leaders' meeting in Shenzhen, with nuclear arms, Ukraine,
   Iran and trade on the agenda — and no breakthrough.
2. **Cyclone Akvan hits India today** — the severe cyclonic storm makes
   landfall around noon on Friday on the Andhra Pradesh–Odisha coast,
   with 90–100 km/h winds, around 50,000 people evacuated and schools
   shut across the coastal districts.
3. **World Court: Myanmar guilty of genocide** — the International Court
   of Justice in The Hague ruled that Myanmar's military intended to
   destroy a substantial part of the Rohingya minority in the 2017
   crackdown.
4. **Riyadh tower struck as Gulf war widens** — a Houthi strike hit
   Riyadh's tallest skyscraper, the Saudi sovereign wealth fund's
   headquarters, and a tanker was hit near Oman as attacks on shipping
   in the Strait of Hormuz continued.
5. **Anthropic's $2 trillion debut** — the Claude maker's shares opened
   34% above their $83 IPO price at $111.50 on Nasdaq, briefly valuing
   the company at about $2 trillion in the largest IPO on record
   ($81.3 billion raised), closing at $97.17, up 17%.
